# Opskrifter til blindgyder — bunke deadend-b

16 elementer. Hvert af dem indgår i dag i NUL opskrifter:
spilleren får dem som belønning, og de fører ingen steder hen.

Skriv **mindst 2** nye opskrifter pr. element (gerne 3).

## De 25 mest brugte partnere (foretræk disse — de er allerede i spillet)

- `nabo` Neighbour (person, alive)
- `ild` Fire (phenomenon, hot)
- `sten` Stone (material, hard/heavy/blunt)
- `hulemaleri` Cave painting (abstract, sacred)
- `knogle` Bone (material, hard/dead/light)
- `baer` Berries (material, edible/light)
- `skind` Hide cloak (material, insulating/dead/soft)
- `pind` Stick (material, dry/light)
- `vand` Water (material, wet)
- `stenoekse` Stone axe (tool, sharp/hard/weapon)
- `keramik` Pottery (tool, hard/fragile/vessel)
- `rullesten` Round stone (material, hard/heavy/blunt)
- `reb` Rope (material, light/portable/dry)
- `graes` Dry grass (material, dry/light/insulating)
- `mudder` Mud (material, soft/wet/sticky)
- `stamme` Log (material, heavy/dry)
- `bautasten` Standing stone (structure, hard/heavy/sacred)
- `fjer` Feather (material, light/soft/dead)
- `sne` Snow (phenomenon, cold/wet)
- `dyr` Wild boar (creature, alive/heavy)
- `planker` Planks (material, dry/light)
- `tromme` Drum (material, loud/dry)
- `grottebryg` Cave brew (food, edible/wet)
- `fugl` Bird (creature, alive/light)
- `hund` Dog (creature, alive/tame)
