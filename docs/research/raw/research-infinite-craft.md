section. Duplicated results (several recipes for the same target) are intentional.

Caveats:
- Infinite Craft results are LLM-generated and can differ over time; treat recipes as "commonly reported", not guaranteed.
- The tail sections of the gamertweak/gameleap tables contain some descriptive-looking pairs (e.g. "complete + full = whole") of dubious in-game provenance; obviously corrupt rows and any recipe producing one of the four starters were excluded here.
- Multiple recipes for the same result reflect the game's many-to-one nature and are all kept.
